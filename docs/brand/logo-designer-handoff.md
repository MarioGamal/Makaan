# Makaanak logo designer handoff

## Brand direction

Makaanak is an Arabic-first, owner-first residential property marketplace for Cairo. The identity should feel trustworthy, contemporary, grounded, and calm—not overly luxurious, corporate, or broker-led.

Primary Arabic name: **مكانك**  
English name: **Makaanak**

## Logo palette

| Role | Colour | HEX | RGB | Guidance |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Primary | Forest Green | `#125C4E` | 18, 92, 78 | Main symbol or wordmark colour |
| Dark | Deep Green | `#0B463B` | 11, 70, 59 | Dark backgrounds and one-colour variant |
| Wordmark | Deep Ink | `#192A25` | 25, 42, 37 | Recommended dark wordmark |
| Tint | Soft Sage | `#DCECE5` | 220, 236, 229 | Supporting fields and secondary brand graphics |
| Neutral | Warm Canvas | `#F7F4EE` | 247, 244, 238 | Primary warm background |
| Accent | Terracotta | `#B8643B` | 184, 100, 59 | Sparing accent; avoid making it dominant |
| Reverse | White | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | Logo reversed on green or photography |

Recommended default combination: Forest Green symbol (`#125C4E`) with a Deep Ink wordmark (`#192A25`). The reversed version should be solid white on Forest Green or Deep Green.

The following are interface neutrals, not required logo colours: `#FFFDF8`, `#EEE8DC`, `#53615C`, and `#D7D1C5`.

Status colours are functional UI colours and should not enter the logo: Success `#1C7658`, Warning `#A46016`, and Danger `#AE3E32`.

For print production, the designer should convert the approved master colours to CMYK or spot colours using the printer's intended ICC profile rather than relying on an automatic generic conversion.

## Required logo system

Please provide:

1. Arabic primary lockup: symbol + **مكانك**.
2. English lockup: symbol + **Makaanak**.
3. Compact bilingual lockup where practical.
4. Standalone symbol suitable for app icons, favicons, map pins, and avatars.
5. Horizontal and stacked configurations.
6. Full-colour, one-colour green, solid black, and reversed-white versions.
7. Clear-space and minimum-size guidance.

## File delivery

- Editable vector masters: SVG, PDF, and AI or EPS.
- Transparent PNG exports at 1×, 2×, and 4×.
- Favicon/app icon exports: 16, 32, 180, 192, and 512 px.
- Social avatar: 1080 × 1080 px.
- Open Graph/share image template: 1200 × 630 px.
- Colour and monochrome versions on light, dark-green, and photographic backgrounds.

## Typography context

The website currently uses IBM Plex Sans Arabic and Inter Tight. The logo may use custom lettering, but it should remain visually compatible with these families and preserve legibility at small sizes.

Avoid generic roofline/key icons unless the concept is distinctive enough to be ownable. The symbol should still make sense if Makaan later expands beyond property listings into other marketplace categories.
